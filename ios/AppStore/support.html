Session Planner: Support

Session Planner shows your AWS re:Invent reserved seats, invitations and favorites as a calendar. It is an independent app, not affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon Web Services.

Who can sign in

You need an AWS Builder ID that is registered for the event. Without one, choose "Try the sample conference" to see how the app works with made-up sessions.

Common questions

It says my Builder ID is not registered. The Builder ID you signed in with is not registered for this event. Sign out and sign in with the one you registered with.

A session says "Full" when I try to reserve. That answer comes from the conference site. Open Sessions and choose "Seats open" to see sessions that still have seats, and update first: seats change quickly.

Sign-in stopped on a page that cannot connect. This can happen if you leave the app for a while during sign-in. Close the sheet and tap "Sign in" again.

I am asked to sign in again. A saved sign-in lasts up to 30 days, and AWS can end it sooner. Signing in again brings everything back; your schedule lives on the conference site.

How do I remove my information? Settings, then Sign out. See the privacy policy.
